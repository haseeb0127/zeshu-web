# Zeshu Rider Operations SOP

## Purpose

This SOP defines the minimum operating standard for Zeshu riders handling local product deliveries. It is an internal operating procedure, not a public delivery-time guarantee.

## Core rules

1. Safety outranks speed. A rider must never take an unsafe route, violate traffic rules, use a phone unsafely while moving, or continue a delivery during an emergency merely to protect an ETA.
2. A rider may handle only orders assigned through the authorized Zeshu rider workflow.
3. Customer address, phone and order data may be used only to complete the assigned delivery and support that delivery.
4. Never ask a customer for an OTP, card CVV, UPI PIN, password or other account secret.
5. Never collect or promise an off-platform payment unless Zeshu has explicitly launched and documented that payment method.
6. Never mark an order DELIVERED before the handoff is genuinely completed.

## Before starting a shift

- Sign in with the rider's own authorized account.
- Confirm the rider profile is active and not suspended.
- Confirm phone battery, mobile data, GPS permission and navigation are working.
- Confirm the vehicle is roadworthy and legally usable for the assigned delivery work.
- Confirm no previous assigned order is still unresolved.
- Use the rider app/dashboard only on the rider's own device; do not share rider credentials.

## Accepting an assignment

The rider should verify:

- order reference;
- pickup seller/store;
- delivery area;
- package count where shown;
- any handling note that is safe and lawful;
- current order status.

If the pickup, destination or package appears inconsistent with the assigned order, do not improvise. Contact Zeshu Support/Admin before collecting it.

## Pickup procedure

At pickup:

- confirm the order reference with the seller/store;
- verify the visible package count and obvious packaging condition;
- do not open sealed customer goods unless the workflow explicitly requires seller-side verification;
- report obvious leakage, damage, tampering or a clearly wrong package before leaving;
- move the order through the authorized status flow only when the corresponding event actually happened.

If an item/package is missing or visibly damaged, stop and escalate before departure.

## Navigation and GPS

- Keep rider location permission enabled during an active assigned delivery where the rider workflow requires it.
- GPS freshness and ETA are customer-assistance signals, not guarantees.
- Use lawful, safe navigation. Traffic, weather, road closures and safety conditions override the displayed ETA.
- Do not manipulate GPS, fake location updates or mark progress that did not occur.
- If location sharing fails, continue only when the delivery can still be completed safely and contact support if tracking is materially affected.

## Customer contact

Use customer contact only when reasonably necessary for the assigned delivery, for example:

- locating the entrance;
- clarifying a safe handoff point;
- informing the customer of a material access problem.

Do not:

- use the customer's number for personal contact;
- copy customer data outside the delivery workflow;
- ask for account/payment secrets;
- pressure the customer to move payment or communication off Zeshu.

## Handoff

At the destination:

- verify the correct customer/location as reasonably possible;
- hand over the correct package;
- follow any authorized proof-of-delivery step;
- mark DELIVERED only after successful handoff.

If the customer is unavailable, the address cannot be located, access is unsafe, or the customer asks for a materially different destination, do not silently complete the order. Follow the escalation flow.

## Failed or blocked delivery

Use human support/admin review when any of these occurs:

- wrong or incomplete address that cannot be safely resolved;
- customer unreachable after reasonable contact attempts;
- customer requests a different destination outside the current service flow;
- package is damaged, leaking, tampered with or missing;
- seller gives a different order/package;
- delivery appears fraudulent or unsafe;
- rider has a breakdown, accident or medical problem;
- severe weather/road condition makes the route unsafe;
- customer or seller dispute cannot be resolved through the normal workflow.

Do not mark a blocked delivery as DELIVERED.

## Safety incident

For an immediate emergency, the rider should contact local emergency services first where appropriate, then Zeshu Support/Admin when safe to do so.

Examples requiring urgent escalation:

- accident or injury;
- threat, assault or harassment;
- dangerous customer/seller location;
- theft or attempted theft;
- serious vehicle safety failure;
- any situation where continuing would create unreasonable danger.

Zeshu support priority for a safety incident is URGENT.

## Lost, damaged or disputed package

- Stop normal completion.
- Preserve the package and available evidence safely.
- Do not promise a refund or replacement.
- Contact Zeshu Support/Admin.
- Support reviews the order/seller/rider record and decides the customer remedy under the applicable policy.

Lost/damaged parcel or money-related disputes are HIGH priority.

## Order-status discipline

Use only truthful status transitions:

- READY_FOR_PICKUP — order is waiting for collection.
- PICKED_UP — rider has actually collected it.
- OUT_FOR_DELIVERY — the collected order is genuinely on the delivery route.
- DELIVERED — customer handoff is genuinely complete.
- CANCELLED — only through the authorized cancellation path.

Never advance a status to improve metrics or ETA appearance.

## Privacy and security

Riders must not request, record or share:

- OTP;
- password;
- card number or CVV;
- UPI PIN;
- access token;
- internal provider credential.

Customer/order information must not be posted publicly, shared in personal groups, or retained outside the authorized workflow.

## End of shift

Before going offline:

- ensure no active assigned delivery remains unresolved;
- report any unresolved incident;
- stop unnecessary customer contact;
- leave the rider account/device in a secure state.

## Support escalation ownership

- **URGENT:** safety, accident, threat, harassment, assault, danger.
- **HIGH:** payment/refund dispute, lost/damaged parcel, duplicate debit or other money/provider dispute.
- **SERVICE:** delivery, ride, courier/cargo, marketplace, pharmacy, recharge/bill or other operational service issue.
- **NORMAL:** routine account, navigation or informational question.

These priorities control queue ordering. They are not public response-time promises.

## Launch requirement

Before public local-delivery launch, Zeshu still needs a real rider + customer-device field test covering assignment, pickup, GPS freshness, navigation, ETA, handoff and the support escalation path.
