## Business activity and collection flow (proposed marketplace, not a live payout claim)
1. Sellers independently own inventory, price products, provide tax invoices and meet safety/licensing obligations. Zeshu lists eligible products and handles shopping, customer support and marketplace technology.
2. Customer selects seller-backed products; Zeshu validates seller availability, service area, applicable delivery/shipping quote, item price and any separately listed platform charge **before** payment.
3. Once a payment gateway and an approved marketplace split service are contracted, the gateway collects through the legally approved merchant-of-record arrangement, reconciles callbacks and handles linked seller settlements. Zeshu may invoice its own service fees separately. Do not manually promise direct transfers without bank/PSP approval.
4. Vendor payout records must show the seller amount, actual contracted gateway fees, approved seller-funded shipping/promotion, lawful seller-fault claims, any permitted statutory TCS/TDS and refunds/chargebacks, with no hidden automatic deductions.
5. Customer complaint/refund is handled by Zeshu support in coordination with seller and courier; customer statutory rights are not contingent on vendor payout completion.
6. Local delivery via verified Zeshu Move in supported Jagtial areas; customer sees delivery fees. Nationwide courier, COD and cold-chain shipping are disabled until live provider integration/approval/QA.
