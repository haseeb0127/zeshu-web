# Zeshu Pharmacy — verified medical shop onboarding only (24 November 2026)

## Scope built
- Public /pharmacy discovery and truthful no-orders-yet message. Search route on Zeshu home.
- Existing /partners?type=LICENSED_PHARMACY#partner-apply preselects a licensed medical shop, asks for declared drug licence/city and explicit no-prescription acknowledgement.
- Existing protected partner_leads stores applicant submissions. No duplicate lead database, no patient or health data collected.
- New /admin/pharmacy shows ONLY LICENSED_PHARMACY leads to authenticated Zeshu administrators and stores private document/operations review in pharmacy_partner_reviews.
- Reviewer sees nine explicit compliance checks: drug licence, pharmacist registration, premises, district, record/privacy, excluded medicines, cold-chain exclusion, issuing pharmacy's cash memo, signed agreement. All checks are manual claims for operator review; they do not activate sales.
- Suggested commercial proposal 0% commission and ₹299/mo tax-inclusive. No automatic threshold billing, no subscription or plan purchase. Consider a 30-day founding partner free pilot only after terms are signed.

## Absolute disabled boundaries
- NO upload/storage of prescriptions, diagnosis, health history or patient medication list.
- NO H/H1/X drug catalogue or general medicine search checkout.
- NO automated prescription vetting, drug brand substitution, tax invoice, payment or Zeshu Cash reward for medicine sales.
- NO active medication delivery and NO guaranteed 30-minute SLA.
- NO preloading fictional pharmacies or marking unverified vendors as partners.
- NO medicine product advertising targeted at prescription-only drugs.
- Never open the regular customer checkout for medicines by simply adding a category.

## Legal/field checks before any prescription order launch
1. Verify Telangana retail premises Form 20/21 and the specific licensee, permitted categories, operational address, validity and registered pharmacist through the Drugs Control Administration and Telangana Pharmacy Council.
2. Obtain written advice from qualified Telangana pharmacy regulatory counsel / DCA regarding the specific e-commerce/intermediary role of Zeshu, laws and CDSCO's 2020 doorstep-delivery conditions, including same-revenue-district rule, receipt of Schedule H prescriptions, notice/record obligations and exclusions of Schedule H1, X, narcotic, psychotropic and controlled substances from that route. Do not generalize the 2020 doorstep rule into approval for platform transactions.
3. Have the pharmacist review prescriptions and dispense from licensed premises; prohibit substitution without clinician/customer and regulatory authorization. Licensed pharmacy issues the lawful customer cash memo/invoice.
4. No Zeshu drug storage or repackaging. Agree sealed tamper-evident handoff and incident handling; exclude refrigerated temperature-sensitive items until validated cold-chain SOP and insurance.
5. Before any image upload, implement segregated encrypted private storage, short-lived authenticated access, audit trail, documented retention/erasure schedule, explicit privacy notice and approved processor/partner agreements. Do not promise a fixed automatic deletion duration that conflicts with statutory recordkeeping.
6. Confirm applicable GST/e-commerce operator treatment (including invoicing, collection/TCS/TDS if relevant), delivery rider law, medicines returns and recalls, customer grievance escalation, and digital payment settlement with CA/legal counsel. Unregistered/non-operational GST status prevents starting fee charging without review.
7. Only then implement pharmacist portal, prescription upload, quote approval, customer confirmation and lawful payment; test an actual authorized Jagtial pharmacy in controlled pilot before public activation.

## Business economics — illustrative only
At 5 paying pharmacies × ₹299 including 18% GST, revenue excluding output GST is 5 × (299/1.18) = ₹1,266.95, before other costs. 60 orders/shop × ₹500 = ₹30,000/month GMV, but Zeshu's 0% sales commission generates zero percent-of-GMV revenue; delivery charges are not margin. Avoid assuming a flat ₹7 contribution per order before proving rider compensation, payment processing, refunds, customer acquisition and support costs.
